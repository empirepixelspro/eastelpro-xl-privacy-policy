EastelPro XL Android

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EastelPro XL (developed by Empire Pixels Pro) handles information when you use our Android application. EastelPro XL has no backend server — all of your data stays safely on your device. By using the app, you agree to the use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Target Audience & Age Appropriateness

EastelPro XL is intended for an adult audience aged 18 and over — mobile telco sales agents, Eastelpreneur entrepreneurs and shop owners who use the app as a working tool for network speed testing, plan comparison and customer prospect management.

The app is installed and operated on the adult's own phone. It contains no account registration, no child profile and no mechanism for a minor to submit information to us. We do not knowingly collect, use or disclose personal information from anyone under 18.

2. Who Is in Control

One person installs the app and controls it. The customer prospects, notes and status changes you record are simple entries created by you on that same device. You can edit or delete any of them at any time from within the app.

3. Information the App Accesses

The app accesses the following only on your device, and only with your permission where required:

  • Local storage: your Eastelpreneur profile, customer prospect records, the official Eastel plan catalog, FAQ/guide material and settings are written to the app's own on-device database. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Camera & photos (optional): only if you choose an avatar, a referral QR image, or attach a testimonial / infographic. The image is compressed to WebP and stored locally.
  • Internet: used only to run the network speed test on your device, to load advertisements, and to process Google Play in-app purchases.
  • What we do NOT collect: we do not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data, contacts, precise location, call logs, or unique device identifiers to any server we operate — the app has no backend of its own.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To run the network speed test and display the result on your screen.
  • To generate plan comparisons, savings figures and ready-made sales scripts that you review before sharing.
  • To keep your prospect list, statuses and follow-up history on the device.
  • To share a message through WhatsApp only when you press the send button — the message is handed to the WhatsApp app, never to us.
  • To process your optional PRO upgrade securely through Google Play Billing — payment details are never seen or stored by us.

5. Advertising

EastelPro XL may display Google AdMob advertisements. Google may collect advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's own privacy policies. You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. No other data is shared with advertisers. Ads are shown only in the free version; upgrading to PRO removes them.

6. In-App Purchases

Optional PRO upgrades are sold as one-time in-app products and are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive, process or store your card or payment details, and we do not operate any payment system of our own.

7. Data Security & Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. Any network traffic the app makes — speed tests, advertisements and Play purchases — is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Data Deletion: you retain full control. Delete individual prospects from the prospect list, use the in-app reset options to clear the catalogs, export a JSON backup first if you want to keep a copy, or simply uninstall the app — all records are removed with it. To request removal of any technical support records, contact us by email with your identifier.
